How the call is made

Three steps, every fixture — no guesswork.

01

Read the signals

Season form, head-to-head records, player form and absences, plus stadium weather — 34 weighted inputs per fixture.

02

Simulate the match

A gradient-boosted model projects each team's match stats, then turns expected goals into win, draw and loss probabilities.

03

Rank the call

Hundreds of weight configurations are scored against real results; the balanced best becomes the published prediction.
